# Insight Quality Reference

Useful insights change a decision. They do not merely summarize files.

For each top insight, include:

- Evidence: saved file, graph output, repeated note, or dated observation.
- Implication: what this changes about priorities, risk, or execution.
- Recommended action: concrete next move.
- Cost of ignoring: what gets worse if nothing changes.
- Confidence: high, medium, or low.

Prefer evidence-backed non-obvious implications over broad coverage.
